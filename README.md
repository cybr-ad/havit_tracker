## 📋 Overview

**Habit Tracker Pro** is a detailed, spreadsheet-style habit tracker for people who need more than basic checkboxes. Instead of just marking a task as "done," you can log exact numbers—like liters of water drank or hours worked—and instantly see your daily, monthly, and yearly progress.

It runs offline on your computer, so your data stays private and is never sent to the cloud. To keep your information safe, it requires a 16-character password and emails you a 6-digit login code. You can use it right on your own device, or host it on a remote server to track your habits from anywhere.

It is a massive step up from a standard spreadsheet. With deep stats, a built-in focus timer, and a gaming-style leveling system, **Habit Tracker Pro** is your personal, secure dashboard for building real discipline.
