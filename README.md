## 📋 Overview

**HAVIT Pro** is a detailed, spreadsheet-style habit tracker built for people who want more than just simple checkboxes. Instead of just marking a habit as "done," you can track exact numbers—like liters of water drank or hours worked—and instantly see your daily, monthly, and yearly progress.

It runs completely offline on your own machine, meaning your data stays private and is never sent to the cloud. To keep your information secure, it requires a 16-character password and verifies your login with a real 6-digit code sent to your email. Once the sever is running locally you can access it by local device or host to a remote server and monitor your habit regularly. It is thousand time better than basic spreadsheet checkboxes. By combining deep stats, a built-in focus timer, and a gaming-style leveling system, HAVIT Pro acts as your personal, secure dashboard for building discipline.
